Privacy Policy - One Square

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What data do we collect?

One Square uses information from your PLIXO account to provide login, player name and skin synchronization features. When you enter your 20-character account code, the extension checks it using Firebase Realtime Database. It retrieves your account name, device identifier and selected skin information.

Local storage

The extension stores your account code, account name, device identifier, player identifier, selected skin and game preferences locally in your browser. Your single-player world and hotbar are also stored locally. This information remains until the extension data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

Multiplayer

When you enter multiplayer, One Square uses Firebase Realtime Database to synchronize game information. This includes your player identifier, display name, in-game X/Y position, timestamp, online status and selected skin image. Changes to the shared multiplayer world are also sent to Firebase so other players can see them.

Other players in the same multiplayer environment may see your display name, position and selected skin. Your live player, online-status and skin entries are set to be removed when you leave multiplayer or when your database connection is disconnected. Shared world changes may remain available to other players.

Third-party services

One Square uses Google Firebase Realtime Database to verify PLIXO account codes, retrieve profile and skin information, and provide multiplayer functionality. The extension communicates with Firebase over HTTPS.

For more information about Google's data practices, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

How we use your data

The information described above is used to provide account login, remember your account, display your player name and skin, and operate multiplayer features. One Square does not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

PLIXO login is required to use the current version of the extension. You can clear locally stored extension data or uninstall the extension to remove information stored in your browser. Multiplayer information is shared with other players as described above.

Children's privacy

One Square is a general-audience game. The extension does not intentionally request your real name, email address, postal address or precise location. Do not use personal information as your in-game display name.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how One Square handles information, you can contact the developer through GitHub.
