Privacy Policy - Mini Browser Platformer

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What data do we collect?

Mini Browser Platformer includes two games: Mini Browser and Mini Spikes. The extension uses information from your PLIXO account to provide player name and skin synchronization features. When you enter your 20-character account code, the extension uses Firebase Realtime Database to retrieve the associated account and skin information.

When you use the games, the extension may also store and synchronize game scores, completion times and race trophies associated with your account.

Local storage

The extension stores your PLIXO account code and relevant game preferences locally in your browser. Game-related information, such as locally saved high scores, may also be stored locally. This information remains until the extension data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

Multiplayer

When you enter a multiplayer game, Mini Browser Platformer uses Firebase Realtime Database to synchronize game information. This may include your player identifier, display name, in-game position, online status, timestamps and selected skin information.

Other players in the same multiplayer environment may see your display name, in-game position and selected skin. Multiplayer information is used to provide the real-time multiplayer experience.

High scores and race trophies

Mini Browser Platformer uses Firebase Realtime Database to store and synchronize game high scores, completion times and race trophies. These records may be associated with your PLIXO account code and player name so that your progress can be recognized across sessions.

Third-party services

Mini Browser Platformer uses Google Firebase Realtime Database to retrieve PLIXO account and skin information, synchronize multiplayer gameplay, and store game scores and race trophies. The extension communicates with Firebase over HTTPS.

For more information about Google's data practices, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

How we use your data

The information described above is used only to provide the games, remember your account, display your player name and selected skin, synchronize multiplayer gameplay, and save game progress and scores. Mini Browser Platformer does not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can clear locally stored extension data or uninstall the extension to remove information stored in your browser. Account-related synchronization and multiplayer features use Firebase as described above.

Children's privacy

Mini Browser Platformer is a general-audience game. The extension does not intentionally request your real name, email address, postal address or precise location. Do not use personal information as your in-game display name.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how Mini Browser Platformer handles information, you can contact the developer through GitHub.
